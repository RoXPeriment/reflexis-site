Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Rovid Publisher is used only by its owner, to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

  • What it stores: the sign-in keys each platform issues for the owner's accounts, and a record of what was posted (post IDs and links). Both are kept only on the owner's own computer.
  • What it collects from others: nothing. It doesn't read, collect or store any information about other people, viewers or followers.
  • What it shares: the videos and captions it posts go to the platform they are posted on. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke the app's access at any time in each platform's account settings.
